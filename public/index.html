Sipat: Gabay kahit walang signal

Sipat is an offline-first typhoon companion for Filipino households on budget Android phones: sourced answers in Taglish, fitted to your family, with no signal.

How it works

  • Three bagyo phases. Bago ang bagyo, Habang may bagyo and Pagkatapos ng bagyo each change the theme, the checklist and the answers.
  • Tap the warning you heard on the radio. PAGASA wind signal 1 to 5, rainfall, storm surge or gale, and get a checklist fitted to your household.
  • Ask in Taglish. Answers come only from official guides bundled in the app (PAGASA, NDRRMC and OCD, DOH, PHIVOLCS, BFP), with the source one tap away. When nothing matches: "Wala ito sa gabay."
  • Useful without AI. The vetted summary shows first; Llama 3.2 1B on the phone puts it in plain words second.
  • Private by design. No account needed, no analytics. Family names, photos and location stay on the phone.

Households never pay; LGUs pay for a localized pack. Source code and docs: github.com/Jimuelle07/Sipat. Product film: YouTube.
